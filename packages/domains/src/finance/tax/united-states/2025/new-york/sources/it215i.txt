Online users only
The worksheets below are interactive calculators. To use the
calculators:
1. In the yellow highlighted fields, enter the amounts requested in
   the instructions for each line.
2. Once you have correctly entered your information, select
   Calculate.
3. If you need to start over, select Reset.
4. When finished, continue to Worksheet C, New York City earned
   income credit.
