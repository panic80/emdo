        <h2 id="h-1">New for&nbsp;2025</h2>
<h3>Tax rates and income brackets</h3>
<p>The personal income levels used to calculate your Ontario tax have&nbsp;changed.</p>
<h3>Credits and benefits</h3>
<p>The amounts for most provincial <span class="nowrap">non-refundable</span> tax credits have&nbsp;increased.</p>
<p>The Ontario minimum tax carryover rate has been reduced.</p>
<p>A new refundable fertility treatment tax credit of 25% of eligible expenses up to $20,000, for a maximum credit of $5,000, was&nbsp;introduced.</p>

    


</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section">
    

    
        <h2 id="h-2">Ontario benefits for individuals and families</h2>
<p>To make sure you get your payments on time, you and your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner need to file your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-r.html"><span class="nowrap">2025 income</span> tax and benefit return</a>(s) by <span class="nowrap">April&nbsp;30</span>, 2026. The Canada Revenue Agency&nbsp;(CRA) will use the information from your return(s) to calculate the payments you are entitled to get from these programs:</p>
<dl class="dl-horizontal dt-max">
<dt>Ontario child benefit</dt> <dd><p>The Ontario child benefit&nbsp;(OCB) is a <span class="nowrap">non-taxable</span> amount paid to help <span class="nowrap">low-</span> to <span class="nowrap">moderate-income</span> families provide for their children. The&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario child benefit">OCB</abbr> and the <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit.html">Canada child benefit</a>&nbsp;(CCB) are delivered together in one monthly&nbsp;payment.</p>
<p>To receive the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario child benefit">OCB</abbr>, you and your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner need to file your income tax and benefit return(s) and be eligible for the <abbr title="Canada child benefit">CCB</abbr>. You only need to apply once in a lifetime for each child under the <span class="nowrap">age of&nbsp;18</span> in your&nbsp;care.</p>
<p>If you are new to Canada or returning to Canada, call the&nbsp;<abbr title="Canada Revenue Agency">CRA</abbr> at&nbsp;<strong><span class="nowrap">1-800-387-1193</span></strong> for specific instructions on how to&nbsp;apply.</p>
<p>If you are the parent of a newborn, you can apply for child benefits when you register your child's birth using Ontario's newborn registration service at <a href="https://www.ontario.ca/page/register-birth-new-baby" rel="external">Register a birth (new&nbsp;baby)</a>.</p>
<p>If you are not registering a newborn, you can apply online through the <abbr title="Canada Revenue Agency">CRA</abbr>'s <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/cra-login-services.html">My Account</a>&nbsp;by selecting &quot;Apply for child benefits&quot; or by completing <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/rc66.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;RC66</span>, <em>Canada Child Benefit Application</em></a>.</p>
<p>If you have applied for child benefits through Ontario's newborn registration service, do not <span class="nowrap">re-apply</span> online or complete <span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;RC66</span> for your child. <span class="nowrap">Re-applying</span> may cause a delay in processing your application and issuing payments.</p>
<p>The <abbr title="Ontario child benefit">OCB</abbr> is fully funded by the <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/provincial-territorial-programs/province-ontario.html">Province of&nbsp;Ontario</a>. For more information about this program, call the <abbr title="Canada Revenue Agency">CRA</abbr> at&nbsp;<strong><span class="nowrap">1-800-387-1193</span></strong>, or by teletypewriter (TTY) at&nbsp;<span class="nowrap"><strong>1-800-665-0354</strong></span> or Video Relay Service (Canada&nbsp;VRS) at&nbsp;<span class="nowrap"><strong>1-800-561-6393</strong></span>.</p>
</dd> <dt id="oof">Ontario opportunities fund</dt><dd><p>The Ontario opportunities fund gives Ontario taxpayers a chance to directly reduce the Province's debt. If you want to contribute to the Ontario opportunities fund from your <span class="nowrap">2025 tax refund</span>, complete the &quot;Ontario opportunities fund&quot; section on the last page of your&nbsp;return.</p>
<p>You will be issued a receipt that can be used with your <span class="nowrap">2026 return</span>. <li><details id="line-75"> <summary class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Line&nbsp;75 – Reduction for dependent children born in&nbsp;2007 <span class="nowrap">or&nbsp;later</span></summary><p>Enter on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;60969</span> the number of dependent children you have. Claim $544 for each dependent&nbsp;child.</p>
<p>If the child has a mental or physical impairment, claim an additional $544 for that dependant on <a href="#line-76"><span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;76</span></a>.</p>
<p class="h4">Dependent&nbsp;child</p>
<p>A dependent child is a person who met <strong>all</strong> of the following conditions:</p>

</ul>

</ul>
<p>If you are preparing a return for a resident of Ontario who died in&nbsp;2025, you can claim this credit on their final&nbsp;return.</p>
<p>If you were bankrupt at any time in&nbsp;2025, you <strong>cannot</strong> claim this tax credit on any&nbsp;return for a tax year ending <span class="nowrap">in&nbsp;2025</span>.</p>
<p class="h4">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>

<li><details id="line-87"> <summary class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Line&nbsp;87 – Community food program donation tax credit for&nbsp;farmers</summary><p>You can claim the community food program donation tax credit for farmers if <strong>all</strong> of the following conditions&nbsp;apply:</p>
<ul>
<li>You were a resident of Ontario at the end of the&nbsp;year</li>
<li>You (or your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner) were a&nbsp;farmer</li>
<li>You made a qualifying donation to an eligible community food program in the&nbsp;year</li>
<li>You claimed the qualifying donation on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;34000</span> of your federal <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/5000-s9.html"><span class="nowrap">Schedule&nbsp;9</span>, <em>Donations and Gifts</em></a>, and on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;58969</span> of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-c.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON428</span></a> as a charitable donation or gift for&nbsp;the&nbsp;year</li>
</ul>
<p>A <strong>qualifying donation</strong> is a donation of one or more agricultural products produced in Ontario and donated to an eligible community food program in&nbsp;Ontario.</p>
<p>An <strong>agricultural product</strong> is any of the&nbsp;following:</p>
<ul>
<li>meat or meat&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">by-products</span></li>
<li>eggs or dairy&nbsp;products</li>
<li>fish</li>
<li>fruits, vegetables, grains, pulses, herbs, nuts, or&nbsp;mushrooms</li>
<li>honey or maple&nbsp;syrup</li>
<li>anything else that is grown, raised, or harvested on a farm and can legally be sold, distributed, or offered for sale at a place other than the producer's premises as food in&nbsp;Ontario</li>
</ul>
<p>An item of any of these types that has been processed is an agricultural product if it was processed only to the extent necessary to be legally sold away from the producer's premises as food intended for human consumption. Items that have been processed beyond this point, such as pickles, preserves and sausages, are not agricultural&nbsp;products.</p>
<p>An <strong>eligible community food program</strong> is a registered charity under the <a href="https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/" rel="external"><em>Income Tax Act</em></a> that meets <strong>one</strong> of the following conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>It distributes food to the public without charge in Ontario and does so mainly to provide relief to persons experiencing poverty (food banks meet this&nbsp;condition)</li>
<li>It operates or oversees one or more student nutrition programs</li>
</ul>
<p>The amount of qualifying donations can be split between a spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner. However, the total amount of qualifying donations cannot be more than the total of the qualifying donations made by both of them in the&nbsp;tax&nbsp;year.</p>
<div class="alert alert-info"><p>You (or your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner) must have had farming income in the year that the donations were&nbsp;made.</p>
<p>You do not have to claim the donations made in&nbsp;2025 on your <span class="nowrap">2025 return</span>. You (or your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner) can carry them forward and claim the credit for the next five years even if neither of you had farming income in the years the donations are&nbsp;claimed.</p>
<p>No matter when you claim the donations, you can only claim each donation&nbsp;once.</p>
<p>For more information, see <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002.html"><span class="nowrap">Guide&nbsp;T4002</span>, <em><span class="nowrap">Self-employed</span> Business, Professional, Commission, Farming, and Fishing Income</em></a>.</p>
</div>
<p>If you are preparing a return for a person who died in&nbsp;2025, you can claim this credit on their&nbsp;final&nbsp;return.</p>
<p>If you were bankrupt in&nbsp;2025, claim your community food program donation tax credit on either <span class="nowrap">the pre-</span> or <span class="nowrap">post-bankruptcy</span> return you file for the tax year ending <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31, 2025</span>, depending on when the qualifying donations were made. If qualifying donations are claimed on more than one return, the total amount of donations that can be claimed on all returns filed for the year cannot be more than the total qualifying donations&nbsp;made.</p>
<p class="h4">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>
<p>Enter on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;62150</span> the amount of donations you have included on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;34000</span> of your federal <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/5000-s9.html"><span class="nowrap">Schedule&nbsp;9</span></a> that are qualifying donations for the community food program donation tax credit for farmers. Then enter 25% of this amount on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;87</span> of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-c.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON428</span></a>.</p>
</details></li>
<li><details> <summary id="line-89" class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Line&nbsp;89 – Ontario health&nbsp;premium</summary><p>You have to pay the Ontario health premium if <strong>both</strong> of the following conditions apply:</p>

<li>Your taxable income on <span class="nowrap"><a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-26000-taxable-income.html">line&nbsp;26000</a></span> of your return is <strong>more <span class="nowrap">than&nbsp;$20,000</span></strong></li>

                    your family's net income on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;8</span> of <span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON479</span>.
                </p>
                <p>If you are preparing a return for a resident of Ontario who died in 2025 and who was
                    69&nbsp;years of age or older on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31</span>, 2024, you can
                    claim this credit on their final return if they meet all of the other conditions above. In that
                    situation, do not enter the surviving spouse's or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span>
                    partner's net income on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;8</span> of <span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON479</span>, unless the person died on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31,&nbsp;2025</span>.</p>
                <p>If you were bankrupt at any time in 2025, you <strong>cannot</strong> claim this credit on any
                    return for a tax year ending in&nbsp;2025.</p>
                <p class="h4">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>
                <p>Complete the calculation on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;63095</span> of your <span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON479</span>.</p>
            </details>

        <li>
            <details>
                <summary class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Ontario seniors' public
                    transit tax&nbsp;credit</summary>
                <p>You can claim the Ontario seniors' public transit tax credit (OSPTTC) if <strong>all</strong> of
                    the following conditions&nbsp;apply:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>You were 65&nbsp;years or older on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31,&nbsp;2024</span>
                    </li>
                    <li>You were a resident of Ontario at the <span class="nowrap">end of&nbsp;the&nbsp;year</span>
                    </li>
                    <li>You paid for eligible public transit services that you used <span class="nowrap">in&nbsp;2025</span></li>
                </ul>
                <p>An <strong>eligible public transit service</strong> is one that is operated by the Government of
                    Ontario or one of its municipalities and meets <strong>all</strong> of the following conditions:
                </p>
                <ul>
                    <li>It is a <span class="nowrap">short-haul</span> service that an individual typically uses for
                        a <span class="nowrap">single-day</span> return&nbsp;trip</li>
                    <li>It is offered to the general&nbsp;public</li>
                    <li>It is operated by bus, subway, train, or&nbsp;tram</li>
                </ul>
                <div class="alert alert-info mrgn-tp-md">
                    <p>Specialized transit services that are designed to transport people with disabilities are also
                        eligible even if they are not offered to the general public or are offered by means other
                        than bus, subway, train, or tram.</p>
                </div>
                <p>You can claim the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario seniors’ public transit tax credit">OSPTTC</abbr> for
                    qualifying payments you made for the use of eligible public transit services <span class="nowrap">in&nbsp;2025</span>.</p>
                <p>A <strong>qualifying payment</strong> is an amount paid for any of the&nbsp;following:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>a public transit pass for a set number of rides during a period of at least one&nbsp;day
                    </li>
                    <li>a public transit pass for an unlimited number of&nbsp;rides</li>
                    <li>an electronic payment&nbsp;card</li>
                    <li>a <span class="nowrap">single-use</span> ticket or token, if a receipt was&nbsp;issued</li>
                    <li>cash fare for specialized transportation services offered to people with disabilities if a
                        receipt was&nbsp;issued</li>
                </ul>
                <div class="alert alert-info mrgn-tp-md">
                    <p>A cash payment made in a fare box for a single ride is only a qualifying payment if it is
                        used for a specialized transit service that is designed to transport people
                        with&nbsp;disabilities.</p>
                </div>
                <p>You can only claim the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario seniors’ public transit tax credit">OSPTTC</abbr>
                    for a senior fare; however,
                    if the transit service you use does not offer a senior rate for the type of pass or ticket you
                    purchase, you can claim a&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">non-senior</span>&nbsp;fare.</p>
                <p>Only you can claim the cost of your public transit services. Your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner <strong>cannot</strong> claim these&nbsp;expenses.
                </p>
                <p>You must reduce the amount of your qualifying payments by the amount of any reimbursements,
                    allowances, or any other form of assistance you received, are entitled to receive, or may
                    reasonably expect to receive, unless the amount is included in your income for any tax year and
                    you did not deduct the amount anywhere else on your&nbsp;return.</p>
                <p class="h4">Receipts</p>
                <p>The following rules apply to receipts for <span class="nowrap">single-use</span> tickets and
                    tokens, and to cash fares for a specialized transit&nbsp;service:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>The receipt must show the name of the transit operator, the date of purchase, and
                        the&nbsp;amount&nbsp;paid</li>
                    <li>If your receipt shows your name, no additional information is&nbsp;needed</li>
                    <li>If the receipt does not show your name, you will need a credit card or bank statement in
                        your name that records the payment for the tickets or&nbsp;tokens</li>
                </ul>
                <p>If you were bankrupt at any time in 2025, you <strong>cannot</strong> claim this tax credit on
                    any return for a tax year ending in&nbsp;2025.</p>
                <p class="h4">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>
                <p>Enter on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;63100</span> the total amount of qualifying payments you
                    made to use eligible public transit services in 2025, up to a maximum of&nbsp;$3,000. Next,
                    enter 15% of this amount on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;14</span> of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tc.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON479</span></a>.</p>
                <p class="h4">Supporting documents</p>
                <p>If you are filing a paper return, do not send any documents. Keep your supporting documents in
                    case you are asked to provide them&nbsp;later.</p>
            </details>
        </li>

                <summary class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Ontario political
                    contribution tax&nbsp;credit</summary>

                    contributed to a registered Ontario political party or constituency association, or to a
                    candidate in an Ontario provincial election in&nbsp;2025.</p>

                    contribution cannot be divided between the two of you if only one receipt was&nbsp;issued.</p>
                <p>If you were bankrupt at any time in 2025, you <strong>cannot</strong> claim this tax credit on
                    any return for a tax year ending in&nbsp;2025.</p>
                <p class="h4">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>
                <p>Enter your total political contributions made in 2025 on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;63110</span>
                    of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tc.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON479</span></a> and calculate the amount to enter on
                    line&nbsp;16 as&nbsp;follows:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li>For contributions of <strong>less than $3,793</strong>, complete the calculation for <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;16</span> using <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-d1.html"><span class="nowrap">Worksheet&nbsp;ON479</span></a></li>

            <details>
                <summary class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Ontario <span class="nowrap">co-operative</span> education tax&nbsp;credit</summary>
                <p>Individuals operating unincorporated businesses may be eligible for the Ontario <span class="nowrap">co-operative</span> education tax credit. Include the amount of credit you
                    claimed for 2025 as <span class="nowrap">self-employment</span> income on your 2026&nbsp;return.

                <p>If you were bankrupt in 2025, your trustee may claim the Ontario <span class="nowrap">co-operative</span> education tax credit if you were eligible for the credit
                    during the period when the trustee acted on your&nbsp;behalf.</p>
                <p>If you hired one or more <span class="nowrap">co-op</span> students enrolled in an Ontario
                    university or college, you may be able to claim a tax credit of up to 30% of eligible
                    expenditures to a maximum of $3,000 per student per qualifying work&nbsp;placement.</p>
                <p class="h4">Eligible expenditures</p>
                <p>Eligible expenditures are reasonable salaries, wages, and other remuneration paid or payable to a
                    student in a qualifying work placement, or a reasonable fee paid or payable to an employment
                    agency for a qualifying work placement. The student must be employed at your permanent
                    establishment in&nbsp;Ontario.</p>
                <p>You must reduce your eligible expenditures by the amount of any government assistance you
                    received, are entitled to receive, or may reasonably expect to receive for
                    the&nbsp;eligible&nbsp;expenditures.</p>
                <p>A <strong>qualifying work placement</strong> is generally four consecutive months ending in the
                    tax year of employment of a student under a qualifying <span class="nowrap">co-operative</span>
                    educational program of an eligible educational&nbsp;institution.</p>
                <p>The minimum employment period is <span class="nowrap">10&nbsp;consecutive</span> weeks. The
                    maximum employment period is <span class="nowrap">16&nbsp;consecutive&nbsp;months</span>.</p>
                <p>The eligible educational institution must give you a certificate for each qualifying work
                    placement for each&nbsp;student.</p>
                <p class="h4">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>
                <p>You can claim up to $3,000 for eligible expenditures for each qualifying work placement ending
                    in&nbsp;2025.</p>
                <p>If the total salaries and wages paid in the previous tax year&nbsp;were:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li><strong>$600,000 or more</strong>, claim 25% of eligible expenditures for the qualifying
                        work&nbsp;placement</li>
                    <li><strong>$400,000 or less</strong>, claim 30% of eligible expenditures for the qualifying
                        work&nbsp;placement</li>
                    <li><strong>more than $400,000 but less than $600,000</strong>, complete the calculation for
                        <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;20</span> using <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-d1.html"><span class="nowrap">Worksheet&nbsp;ON479</span></a>
                    </li>
                </ul>
                <p>Enter your claim on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;20</span> of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tc.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON479</span></a>.</p>
                <p>If you repaid government assistance in 2025 that was related to eligible expenditures for this
                    tax credit in a previous year, the amount of assistance you repaid will qualify for the tax
                    credit for&nbsp;2025.</p>
                <p>The tax credit is equal to the amount of government assistance repaid multiplied by the tax
                    credit rate for the year the eligible expenditures were reduced by the&nbsp;assistance.</p>
                <p>For more information about this tax credit, go to <a href="https://www.ontario.ca/page/co-operative-education-tax-credit" rel="external"><span class="nowrap">Co-operative</span>&nbsp;education tax credit</a> or call the Ontario
                    Ministry of Finance <span class="nowrap">at&nbsp;<strong>1-866-ONT-TAXS</strong></span><strong>
                        (<span class="nowrap">1-866-668-8297</span>)</strong> or by teletypewriter (TTY) <span class="nowrap">at&nbsp;<strong>1-800-263-7776</strong></span>.</p>
                <p class="h4">Supporting documents</p>
                <p>If you are filing a paper return, do not send any documents. Keep your supporting documents in
                    case you are asked to provide them&nbsp;later.</p>
            </details>

<li><details> <summary id="P5_otb" class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Ontario trillium benefit&nbsp;(OTB)</summary><p>The <abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr> includes the following&nbsp;credits:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="#P5_ostc">Ontario sales tax&nbsp;credit</a></li>
<li><a href="#P5_oeptc">Ontario energy and property tax&nbsp;credit</a></li>
<li><a href="#P5_noec">Northern Ontario energy&nbsp;credit</a></li>
</ul>
<p>You must be eligible for <strong>at least one</strong> of these credits to receive the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr>.</p>
<p>The payments for these three credits are combined and delivered monthly.</p>
<p>The <span class="nowrap">2026&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr></span> payments, calculated based on the information provided on your and your spouse's or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner's 2025 return(s) and <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tg.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span></a>, will be issued monthly from <span class="nowrap">July&nbsp;2026</span> to <span class="nowrap">June&nbsp;2027</span>. However, you can choose to wait until <span class="nowrap">June&nbsp;2027</span> to get your <span class="nowrap">2026&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr></span> entitlement in one payment at the end of the benefit year instead of receiving monthly payments from <span class="nowrap">July&nbsp;2026</span> to <span class="nowrap">June&nbsp;2027</span>. To choose this option, tick the box on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61060</span> of your&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span>.</p>
<div class="alert alert-info"><p>If your <span class="nowrap">2026&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr></span> is <strong>$360 or less</strong>, the election above does not apply and your entitlement will be issued in one payment in <span class="nowrap">July&nbsp;2026</span>.</p>
</div>
<p>You may not be eligible for a payment if you are confined to a prison or a similar institution for a period of <span class="nowrap">90&nbsp;days</span> or more that includes the first day of the payment&nbsp;month.</p>
<p>To estimate the amount of <abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr> you may be entitled to, use the <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/child-family-benefits-calculator.html">Child and family benefits calculator</a>.</p>
<p>For more information about the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr>, go to <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/provincial-territorial-programs/province-ontario.html#tb">Ontario trillium benefit&nbsp;(OTB)</a> or call the&nbsp;<abbr title="Canada Revenue Agency">CRA</abbr> at&nbsp;<span class="nowrap"><strong>1-877-627-6645</strong></span>.</p>
<p class="h4" id="P5_ostc">Ontario sales tax credit&nbsp;(OSTC)</p>
<p>The&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario sales tax credit">OSTC</abbr> helps <span class="nowrap">low- to</span> <span class="nowrap">moderate-income</span> individuals <span class="nowrap">19&nbsp;years</span> of age and older, and families (including <span class="nowrap">single-parent</span> families) with the sales tax they&nbsp;pay.</p>
<p class="h5"><strong>Eligibility requirements</strong></p>
<p>You may be eligible for the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario sales tax credit">OSTC</abbr> for <strong>any</strong> month of the <span class="nowrap">July&nbsp;2026</span> to <span class="nowrap">June&nbsp;2027</span> benefit year if you are a resident of Ontario at the beginning of that month and you meet <strong>at least one</strong> of the following conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>You will be <span class="nowrap">19&nbsp;years</span> of age or older at the beginning of that&nbsp;month</li>
<li>You had a spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span>&nbsp;partner</li>
<li>You are a parent who lives or previously lived with your&nbsp;child</li>
</ul>
<p>The&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario sales tax credit">OSTC</abbr> is paid as part of the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario trillium benefit">OTB</abbr>. You do not need to apply for the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario sales tax credit">OSTC</abbr>. The&nbsp;<abbr title="Canada Revenue Agency">CRA</abbr> will use the information from your return to determine your eligibility and tell you if you are entitled to receive the&nbsp;credit.</p>
<p class="h4" id="P5_oeptc">Ontario energy and property tax credit&nbsp;(OEPTC)</p>
<p>The&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> helps <span class="nowrap">low- to</span> <span class="nowrap">moderate-income</span> Ontario residents with the sales tax on energy and with property&nbsp;taxes.</p>
<p>The&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> has two components: the&nbsp;<a href="#energy_comp">energy component</a> and the <a href="#propertytax_comp">property tax component</a>. You should apply for the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> if you are eligible for either of these&nbsp;components.</p>
<p class="h5">Eligibility requirements</p>
<p>You may be eligible for the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> for <strong>any </strong>month of the <span class="nowrap">July&nbsp;2026</span> to <span class="nowrap">June&nbsp;2027</span> benefit year if you were a resident of Ontario on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31</span>, 2025, <strong>and</strong> at the beginning of that month, and you meet <strong>at least one</strong> of the following conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>You will be 18&nbsp;years of age or older at the beginning of that&nbsp;month</li>
<li>You had a spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span>&nbsp;partner at the beginning of that&nbsp;month</li>
<li>You are a parent who lives or previously lived with your&nbsp;child</li>
</ul>
<p class="h5">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>
<p>If you meet the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> eligibility requirements, tick the box on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61020</span> and complete <span class="nowrap">Part&nbsp;A</span> and <span class="nowrap">Part&nbsp;B</span> of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tg.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span></a>. Attach your completed <span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span> to your <span class="nowrap">2025&nbsp;tax&nbsp;return</span>.</p>
<p class="h5" id="energy_comp">Energy component</p>
<p>If you meet the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> eligibility requirements, you may qualify for the energy component if you also meet <strong>at least one</strong> of the following conditions for&nbsp;2025:</p>
<ul>
<li>Rent was paid by or for you for your principal residence that was subject to property tax. If you lived in a subsidized housing unit, check with your landlord to find out if property tax was paid for your&nbsp;unit</li>
<li>Property tax for your principal residence was paid by or for&nbsp;you</li>
<li>You lived in a public <span class="nowrap">long-term</span> care home in Ontario and an amount for accommodation was paid by or for&nbsp;you</li>
<li>You lived on a reserve in Ontario and home energy costs were paid by or for you for your principal residence on the&nbsp;reserve</li>
</ul>
<p class="h5" id="propertytax_comp">Property tax&nbsp;component</p>
<p>If you meet the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> eligibility requirements, you may qualify for the property tax component if you also meet <strong>at least one</strong> of the following conditions for&nbsp;2025:</p>
<ul>
<li>Rent was paid by or for&nbsp;you for your principal residence that was subject to property tax. If you lived in a subsidized housing unit, check with your landlord to find out if property tax was paid for your unit</li>
<li>Property tax for your principal residence was paid by or for&nbsp;you</li>
<li>You lived in a designated Ontario university, college, or private school&nbsp;residence</li>
</ul>
<p>A <strong>principal residence</strong> is a housing unit in Ontario that you usually occupy during the year. For example, it can be a house, apartment, condominium, hotel or motel room, mobile home, or rooming&nbsp;house.</p>
<p class="h5">Supporting documents</p>
<p>Keep all of your property tax, rent, or home energy cost receipts (such as electricity and heat receipts) in case you are asked to provide them&nbsp;later.</p>
<p class="h4" id="P5_noec">Northern Ontario energy credit&nbsp;(NOEC)</p>
<p>The&nbsp;<abbr title="Northern Ontario energy credit">NOEC</abbr> helps <span class="nowrap">low- to</span> <span class="nowrap">moderate-income</span> Northern Ontario residents with the higher energy costs they pay living in the&nbsp;north.</p>
<p class="h5">Eligibility requirements</p>
<p>You may be eligible for the&nbsp;<abbr title="Northern Ontario energy credit">NOEC</abbr> for <strong>any</strong> month of the <span class="nowrap">July&nbsp;2026</span> to <span class="nowrap">June&nbsp;2027</span> benefit year if you were a resident of Northern Ontario on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31</span>, 2025, <strong>and</strong> at the beginning of that month, and you meet <strong>at least one</strong> of the following conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>You will be <span class="nowrap">18&nbsp;years</span> of age or older at the beginning of that&nbsp;month</li>
<li>You had a spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span>&nbsp;partner at the beginning of that&nbsp;month</li>
<li>You are a parent who lives or previously lived with your&nbsp;child</li>
</ul>
<p>You must also meet <strong>at least one</strong> of the following conditions for&nbsp;2025:</p>
<ul>
<li>Rent or property tax for your principal residence in Northern Ontario was paid by or for&nbsp;you</li>
<li>You lived in a public <span class="nowrap">long-term</span> care home in Northern Ontario and an amount for accommodation was paid by or for&nbsp;you</li>
<li>You lived on a reserve in Northern Ontario and home energy costs (for example, electricity, heat) were paid by or for you for your principal residence on the&nbsp;reserve</li>
</ul>
<p>A <strong>principal residence</strong> is a housing unit in Ontario that you usually occupy during the year. For example, it can be a house, apartment, condominium, hotel or motel room, mobile home, or rooming&nbsp;house.</p>
<p><strong>Northern Ontario</strong> means the districts of Algoma, Cochrane, Kenora, Manitoulin, Nipissing, <span class="nowrap">Parry Sound</span>, <span class="nowrap">Rainy River</span>, Sudbury (including the City of Greater Sudbury), <span class="nowrap">Thunder Bay</span>, and&nbsp;Timiskaming.</p>
<p class="h5">How to claim this&nbsp;credit</p>
<p>If you meet the&nbsp;<abbr title="Northern Ontario energy credit">NOEC</abbr> eligibility requirements, tick the box on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61040</span> and complete <span class="nowrap">Part&nbsp;A</span> and <span class="nowrap">Part&nbsp;B</span> of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tg.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span></a>. Attach your completed <span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span> to your <span class="nowrap">2025&nbsp;tax&nbsp;return</span>.</p>
<p class="h5">Supporting documents</p>
<p>Keep all of your property tax, rent, or home energy cost receipts (such as electricity and heat receipts) in case you are asked to provide them&nbsp;later.</p>
</details></li>
<li><details> <summary id="P5_oshptg" class="wb-toggle" data-toggle="{&quot;print&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Ontario senior homeowners' property tax grant&nbsp;(OSHPTG)</summary><p>You can apply for the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario senior homeowners' property tax grant">OSHPTG</abbr> for 2026 if, on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31</span>, 2025, <strong>both</strong> of the following conditions&nbsp;applied:</p>
<ul>
<li>You were <span class="nowrap">64&nbsp;years</span> of age or&nbsp;older</li>
<li>You owned and occupied a principal residence in Ontario that you, or someone on your behalf, paid property tax on for&nbsp;2025</li>
</ul>
<p>A <strong>principal residence</strong> is a housing unit in Ontario that you usually occupy during the year. For example, it can be a house, condominium, mobile home, or <span class="nowrap">life-lease</span>&nbsp;home.</p>
<p>Your grant for 2026 is based on the information you provide on your 2025&nbsp;return. You should receive it within four to eight weeks after you receive your notice of assessment. To estimate the amount of <abbr title="Ontario senior homeowners' property tax grant">OSHPTG</abbr> you may be entitled to, use the <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/child-family-benefits-calculator.html">Child and family benefits&nbsp;calculator</a>.</p>
<p class="h4">How to claim this&nbsp;grant</p>
<p>If you meet the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario senior homeowners' property tax grant">OSHPTG</abbr> eligibility requirements, tick the box on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61070</span>. Enter your total amount of property tax paid for 2025 on <span class="nowrap"><a href="#p9l61120">line&nbsp;61120</a></span> in <span class="nowrap">Part&nbsp;A</span> and complete <span class="nowrap">Part&nbsp;B</span>. Attach your completed <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tg.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span></a> to your <span class="nowrap">2025&nbsp;tax&nbsp;return</span>.</p>
<div class="alert alert-info"><p>If your municipality allowed you to defer paying all or some of your 2025 property tax, enter only the amount of property tax actually paid to the municipality for the&nbsp;year.</p>
</div>
<p class="h4" id="P5_appr">Amounts paid for a principal residence in&nbsp;2025</p>
<p>Complete <span class="nowrap">Parts&nbsp;A</span> <span class="nowrap">and&nbsp;B</span> of your <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/tax-packages-years/general-income-tax-benefit-package/ontario/5006-tg.html"><span class="nowrap">Form&nbsp;ON-BEN</span></a> if you are applying for the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr>, the&nbsp;<abbr title="Northern Ontario energy credit">NOEC</abbr>, <span class="nowrap">and/or</span> the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario senior homeowners' property tax grant">OSHPTG</abbr>.</p>
<p class="h4" id="p9l61100">Line&nbsp;61100</p>
<p>Enter on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61100</span> <strong>any</strong> of the following amounts:</p>
<ul>
<li>rent paid if you rented your principal residence in Ontario for&nbsp;2025</li>
<li>property tax paid by you or for you to the owner of a principal residence (who was not you and was not held in trust for you) and that you have not already used in calculating the amount of rent that you&nbsp;paid</li>
<li>rent paid to your landlord if you rented a mobile home and your landlord paid property tax for the home or for the&nbsp;lot</li>
<li>rent paid if you lived in a private <span class="nowrap">long-term</span> care home, hospital, group home, chronic care facility, or a similar institution and the institution paid full property&nbsp;taxes<div class="alert alert-info mrgn-tp-md"><p>A <strong><span class="nowrap">long-term</span> care home</strong> can include a nursing home or a municipal, First Nations, or charitable home for the&nbsp;aged.</p>
<p>If the institution does not break down the cost of room and board (meals, laundering, or other services) on your receipt, you can claim up to 75% of your total payments as rent on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61100</span>.</p>
<p>To enter an amount for <strong>a <span class="nowrap">non-profit</span> <span class="nowrap">long-term</span> care home</strong>, see <a href="#p9l61230"><span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61230</span></a>.</p>
</div>
</li>
<li>rent paid for your principal residence and one acre of land if you were a farmer. For more information on the property tax paid if you are a farmer, see&nbsp;<a href="#p9l61120"><span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61120</span></a></li>
<li>rent paid by you or for you if you lived somewhere else in Ontario for part of the year besides a designated Ontario university, college, or private school&nbsp;residence<br>
(To find out if your residence is designated, go to <a href="https://www.ontario.ca/page/ministry-finance" rel="external">Ministry of Finance</a> or call the Ontario Ministry of Finance at&nbsp;<strong><span class="nowrap">1-866-ONT-TAXS</span></strong> (<strong><span class="nowrap">1-866-668-8297</span></strong>) or by teletypewriter&nbsp;(TTY) at&nbsp;<strong><span class="nowrap">1-800-263-7776</span></strong>.)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do not enter</strong> rent paid for a principal residence, including a mobile home, that is not subject to property&nbsp;tax.</p>
<p class="h4" id="p9l61120">Line&nbsp;61120</p>
<p>Enter on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61120</span> <strong>any</strong> of the following&nbsp;amounts:</p>
<ul>
<li>property tax paid for your principal residence in Ontario for 2025 if you were a&nbsp;homeowner<div class="alert alert-info mrgn-tp-md"><p>If your municipality let you defer paying all or some of your 2025 property tax, enter only the amount of property tax actually paid to the municipality for the&nbsp;year.</p>
</div>
</li>
<li>property tax paid for your principal residence and one acre of land if you were a&nbsp;farmer</li>
<li>total of the property tax paid for your home plus the property tax that your landlord or site owner paid for the lot you leased if you owned and occupied a&nbsp;mobile&nbsp;home<div class="alert alert-info mrgn-tp-md"><p>If the landlord does not provide a breakdown of the property tax paid for the lot and you do not have an assessment for the lot from the Municipal Property Assessment Corporation, you can estimate the property tax based on the fees paid to your landlord or site owner for the lot. It would be reasonable to take 20% of the fees and add that amount to any property tax paid on the&nbsp;mobile&nbsp;home.</p>
</div>
</li>
<li>property tax paid by you or for you if you lived somewhere else in Ontario for part of the year besides a designated Ontario university, college, or private school&nbsp;residence<br>
(To find out if your residence is designated, go to <a href="https://www.ontario.ca/page/ministry-finance" rel="external">Ministry of Finance</a> or call the Ontario Ministry of Finance at&nbsp;<strong><span class="nowrap">1-866-ONT-TAXS</span></strong> (<strong><span class="nowrap">1-866-668-8297</span></strong>) or by teletypewriter&nbsp;(TTY) at&nbsp;<span class="nowrap"><strong>1-800-263-7776</strong></span>.)</li>
</ul>
<p class="h4" id="#p9l61140">Line&nbsp;61140</p>
<p>Tick the box on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61140</span> if you lived in a designated Ontario university, college, or private school&nbsp;residence.<br>
(To find out if your residence is designated, go to <a href="https://www.ontario.ca/page/ministry-finance" rel="external">Ministry of Finance</a> or call the Ontario Ministry of Finance at&nbsp;<span class="nowrap"><strong>1-866-ONT-TAXS</strong></span> (<strong><span class="nowrap">1-866-668-8297</span></strong>) or by teletypewriter&nbsp;(TTY) at&nbsp;<span class="nowrap"><strong>1-800-263-7776</strong></span>.)</p>
<p>You will get $25 for the <a href="#propertytax_comp">property tax component</a> of the&nbsp;<abbr title="Ontario energy and property tax credit">OEPTC</abbr> for the part of the year that you lived in that&nbsp;residence.</p>
<p>If you also lived somewhere else in Ontario for part of the year and the rent <span class="nowrap">and/or</span> property tax was paid by you or for you, see <span class="nowrap">lines&nbsp;<a href="#p9l61100">61100</a></span> <span class="nowrap">and <a href="#p9l61120">61120</a></span>.</p>
<p class="h4" id="p9l61210">Line&nbsp;61210</p>
<p>Enter on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61210</span> the total of the amounts you paid for 2025 for your energy costs (for example, electricity, heat) for your principal residence if you lived on a reserve in&nbsp;Ontario.</p>
<p class="h4" id="p9l61230">Line&nbsp;61230</p>
<p>Enter on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61230</span> your accommodation costs if you lived in <strong>any</strong> of the&nbsp;following:</p>
<ul>
<li>a <span class="nowrap">non-profit</span> <span class="nowrap">long-term</span> care&nbsp;home</li>
<li>a public <span class="nowrap">long-term</span> care home and the institution did not pay full property&nbsp;taxes</li>
</ul>
<div class="alert alert-info mrgn-tp-md"><p>If the institution does not break down the cost of room and board (meals, laundering, or other services) on your receipt, you can claim an amount of up to 75% of your total payments as accommodation costs on <span class="nowrap">line&nbsp;61230</span>.</p>
</div>
<p>Your property tax and rent <strong>cannot</strong> include any of the&nbsp;following:</p>
<ul>
<li>rent paid for a principal residence, including a mobile home, that is not subject to property&nbsp;tax</li>
<li>payments to individuals who are not reporting the payments as rental income on their&nbsp;returns</li>
<li>property tax or rent paid on part of a home you used for rental or business&nbsp;purposes</li>
<li>property tax or rent paid on a second residence, such as a cottage, if you claimed property tax or rent for your principal residence for the&nbsp;same&nbsp;period</li>
</ul>
<p class="h5">Separation</p>
<p>If you and your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner separated during the year but <strong>lived together</strong> on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31</span>, 2025, enter the total amount of rent or property tax paid for the year, including amounts paid by or for each spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner for a period of&nbsp;separation.</p>
<p>If you and your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner separated during the year and <strong>lived apart</strong> on <span class="nowrap">December&nbsp;31</span>, 2025, enter your share of the rent or property tax for the part of the year before the separation, plus your own rent or property tax paid after the&nbsp;separation.</p>
<p class="h5">Shared principal residence</p>
<p>If you shared a principal residence with one or more persons (other than your spouse or <span class="nowrap">common-law</span> partner), enter your share of the rent or property tax you paid for the&nbsp;year.</p>
</details></li>
</ul>
