# Canada/Ontario 2025 personal review workflow

`personal-package.ts` now executes one connected, version-pinned review workflow:

1. Validate a private `FinanceTaxIntake` and its reviewed source facts against the development questionnaire and exact country/subdivision/taxpayer/year/regime/form coverage.
2. Calculate ordinary service-business T2125 income, when the scope is sole proprietor.
3. Feed T4 boxes and calculated business income into the existing deterministic Schedule8 engine. Carry exact CPP deductions/credits/contributions/overpayments into T1.
4. Calculate total/net/taxable income, published federal/Ontario tax columns, BPA and nonrefundable credits, top-up, Ontario surtax/reduction/health premium, single-person LIFT and basic CWB. T2204 determines the EI credit and overpayment from reviewed insurable earnings and premiums.
5. Reconcile the resulting exact working figures with tax withheld and instalments.
6. Reject blocked runs at export, escape formula-leading CSV cells, and produce immutable source/input/rule hashes and form dependencies. A trusted reviewer can stamp the specific run hash and export the working schedules to CSV with source/version/hash provenance.

The development candidate is **not** a registered `FinanceTaxPackageManifest`. Its `enabled`, `complete`, `reportable`, and `registryEligible` fields stay false. Each line now has an explicit paper-field precision result: proven lossless amounts have `reportableAmount` with two decimal places; unresolved fields remain null. Refund/balance-owing reportable outputs are null. The global country registry remains empty. These per-field values do not make the entire return reportable. Private-case persistence and authenticated review/run/export APIs now consume this workflow as incomplete working papers; this does not activate a return package.

## Current candidate coverage

Exact scope: CA / CA-ON / individual or sole-proprietor / 2025 / income-tax-return / `5006-R-E-25_5006-C-E-25`. Candidate version: `2025.1-personal-workflow.30`. Source versions include T2125 E(25), Schedule8 E(25), Schedule9 E(25), Schedule6 E(25), ON428-A(25), CRA line25200 guidance, CRA ITAM Chapter29 loss-order guidance, the 2025 line 34900 donation guidance and the 2025 line 46900 educator guidance.

Full-year domestic Ontario resident aged19–64, single without dependants. At most one ordinary Canadian T4; domestic interest; optionally one Ontario service business with a full calendar-year accrual period, no inventory/cost-of-goods, no professional/commission/farming/fishing classification, partnership, reserves, staff/subcontract costs, capital assets/CCA, vehicle/home-office costs, special expenses or GST/HST quick method. Allowed business inputs are reviewed business portions of advertising, insurance, licences, office/stationery, professional fees, rent and utilities, after applicable sales-tax adjustments. Ordinary charitable donations are also supported through a reviewed Schedule 9 claim and five-year carryforward ledger. These are source amounts, not automatic eligibility classifications from ledger account names.

The questionnaire explicitly excludes other income, Step3 deductions, credits, special taxes, refunds, elections, provincial479 claims, CPP/QPP exceptions, RC210/ACWB and EI special-benefits agreements/PPIP. It accepts only reviewed general non-capital loss balances for 2006–2024 as line25200 carryforward inputs; ABIL, farm/fishing, limited-partnership, net-capital-loss and other carryforward pools remain blocked. Missing or unreviewed/disputed answers, false scope assertions, unknown facts, loss cases and mismatched source-book hashes block evaluation. A source fact's review or authorization must be established by trusted persistence; this pure module cannot grant either. Existing private declaration sources remain unreviewed and cannot automatically enter this workflow.

These attestations are a development boundary, not proof that every statutory exclusion has been exhaustively encoded. Broad Canadian individual, sole-proprietor and corporation/subdivision coverage remains the eventual goal.

## Paper-field precision in workflow version 3

`personal-reporting.ts` adds the versioned `2025-paper-precision.1` policy to the same immutable run hash and CSV export. `personal-precision-evidence.ts` binds the implemented field policies to actual XFA field paths, input/display pictures, keystroke validation and captured public CRA PDF source hashes. The source catalog covers T1, ON428, Schedule 8, Schedule 6, ON428-A, T2125 and T2204; only explicitly reviewed mapped nodes receive an implemented reporting policy.

The official T1 fillable field for line 10100 accepts cents (`num{...9.99}` and `validateKeystrokeCurrency(...,9,false)`), even though RC4018 describes an EFILE dollar-only transport field for that line. This policy targets the public fillable **paper** form. It does not infer a transport transformation. `validateKeystrokeCurrency` delegates to a float validator with two fractional digits; T2125 explicitly uses `validateKeystrokeFloat(...,10,2,...)`. The distinct maximum integer widths are enforced without altering source values.

Lossless copying, addition, subtraction, comparison and caps of already verified cent-valued inputs need no rounding. These operations now produce two-decimal paper-field amounts across the covered T2125 expense/net chain and T1 income transfers, direct wages/interest/withholding/instalments, fixed Ontario BPA, and resolved T2204 branches. Fractional inputs remain fully supported. A single-rate calculation that introduces fractional cents is blocked until its rounding is proven; its dependent fields remain blocked even if their exact unrounded result happens to be cents. Fields without reviewed operation-level/format proof, including current CPP internals, do not gain reportability merely from their final decimal representation.

The seven public fillable PDFs contain a generic `CoreFunctions.roundNumber` utility. Its presence alone does not establish annual tax rounding: inspected references use it for colour conversion, not the tax fields. No half-up/truncation rule was activated. Remaining reporting statuses distinguish `rounding-unproven`, `dependency-unresolved`, `field-proof-missing`, `field-width-exceeded`, and `blocked-input`.

`python3 sources/verify-personal-precision-captures.py` (from this directory, with pypdf installed) checks all seven PDF hashes and reproduces the 1,085-field catalog directly from their XFA template packets. Tests retain cents in a $9,000.05 business income case, verify the CRA $1,900 earnings/$35 premium example, block the subcent T2204 dependency at $2,000.01, and preserve oversized source amounts while blocking field-width encoding. All-return readiness, refund/balance outputs and package activation remain unavailable until unresolved calculations and full form/eligibility proof are complete.
