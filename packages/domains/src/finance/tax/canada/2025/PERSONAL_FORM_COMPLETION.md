The versioned `personal-form-applicability.ts` audit enumerates every field instance in seven source-hashed CRA XFA templates: 1,271 instances, including text, numeric, choice, checkbox and navigation controls. Repeated internal paths retain distinct occurrence numbers. ## Private nonledger input contract

`CanadaPersonalFormInputEnvelopeSchema` requires case ID, taxpayer ID, questionnaire revision, exact working-run hash, and existing versioned `FinanceTaxFact` evidence records. Facts must be reviewed and match the expected type. Missing, unreviewed, duplicate, unknown, cross-taxpayer and changed-revision data cannot satisfy form readiness. Source access and review authority must be enforced by private persistence before calling this pure audit; its reviewed flags do not confer authority.

`PERSONAL_REQUIRED_FORM_FACTS` defines first/last name, mailing address/city/province/postal code, nine-digit SIN/TTN/ITN, correspondence language, current province, citizenship, conditional Elections Canada authorization, email notification choice and email when requested, Ontario contact-sharing consent, and the specified-foreign-property answer. Email/authorization are explicit choices, never inferred. The format checks do not invent a SIN checksum for TTN/ITN or unproven address/name length restrictions.

`PERSONAL_REQUIRED_BUSINESS_FORM_FACTS` additionally identifies business name/address, product/service, six-digit industry code, explicit fiscal dates for the supported full-calendar-year scope, internet site count and internet revenue percentage. Reviewed program-account applicability, the proprietor name and tax-number transfers, explicit last-business-year selection and the conditional five website addresses now have field mappings. Preparer details require the explicit reviewed name-and-address fact described below. No identity or applicability fact is inferred from the ledger. These inputs now use the existing private-case declaration editor, exact source-revision input review, and immutable calculation input snapshot. Business prompts appear only for sole-proprietor cases; raw declarations never become reviewed inputs automatically.

Taxpayer signing is explicitly `manual-unperformed`. A reviewable form can carry a manual signature/date/contact placeholder and instructions. This action does not block deterministic calculation completeness; the audit does not sign or attest on behalf of the taxpayer. Filing and certification are outside scope.
